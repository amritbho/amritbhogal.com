Preparing for interviews is stressful and lonely

In today’s job market, people struggle to prepare well for interviews, which costs them chances and adds to their anxiety. Existing tools rarely give specific advice or a safe space to improve.

Research before features

Market analysis and user research refined the app’s features so they match what candidates expect and where the industry is heading.

Rebuilding confidence after being ghosted

PrepU is an AI-led app to combat interview anxiety. It starts from the emotional toll of being ghosted by recruiters, and helps people rebuild confidence, manage anxiety and go into interviews prepared.

Structure first

Low-fidelity wireframes mapped layout and functionality before any visual design, so the visual work started from a clear structure.

The interface
