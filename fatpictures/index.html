●  About

Made in London. Built to travel

Fat Pictures is a development and production company based in London. Our slate is mainly feature films, with high-end drama series alongside. Thrillers of all stripes are our preferred genre, though the current slate also holds a high-concept comedy, an elevated psychological drama and a contained sci-fi.
